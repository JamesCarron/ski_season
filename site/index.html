Extras

  • Book apartments first. Italian and Andorran stock is thin (21–36 listings).
  • Fancy-dress day on Thursday. Spa day midweek, and bring flip-flops.
  • Lessons: 5 morning group lessons for those who need them.
  • Insurance with winter-sports and third-party cover. EHIC/GHIC is not enough.
  • January is cold. Bring proper layers, a buff and goggles for flat light.
