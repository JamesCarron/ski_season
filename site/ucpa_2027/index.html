Getting there

Each option lists the airports to fly into, with driving time, and shows the best-value airport for each week. Return fares, Saturday to Saturday, from Google Flights on 12–13 Oct 2026.

🇮🇪 From Dublin

Tignes, Val d'Isère, Val Thorens, La Plagne, Les ArcsGeneva, Lyon or Grenoble, 2–3 h drive. Geneva is the best value in most weeks, Lyon in the week of 30 Jan. Grenoble (Ryanair direct) has the shortest drive to Les Arcs and La Plagne but costs more. Chambéry is closest but has no Dublin flights
Serre ChevalierTurin (~2 h drive) is the best value every week. Grenoble is the same distance but costs more
Flaine, ArgentièreGeneva (1h15–1h30 drive)

🇨🇭 From Zurich

Argentière: direct train RecommendedVia Martigny and the Mont-Blanc Express, ~4–4.5 h. The station is next to the centre, so no transfer is needed
Tarentaise resorts and Flaine: train to Geneva Airport RecommendedDirect, ~3 h, CHF 25–90 each way, then the same transfer as the Dublin group. Trains all the way to Moûtiers or Bourg-St-Maurice take 6h40–7h15, which is no quicker
Serre Chevalier: fly to Turin1-stop, €192–€262 return, then ~2 h drive. The train is ~8 h via Milan and Oulx
